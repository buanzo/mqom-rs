# Code of conduct

Participation must remain professional, technically honest, and respectful.

- Critique claims, code, specifications, and evidence rather than people.
- Disclose uncertainty, conflicts of interest, and limits of test evidence.
- Do not exaggerate the implementation's security, audit status, or readiness.
- Do not harass, threaten, expose private information, or discriminate against
  participants.
- Coordinate vulnerability disclosure through the private security channel.

The repository steward may edit or remove material and restrict participation
when necessary to protect contributors, users, or the integrity of the
research record. Serious concerns may be raised privately through the
repository owner's public GitHub profile.
